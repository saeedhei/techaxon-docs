# Before Starting Bigger Changes

When you have a new idea, feature, or a change that touches multiple files, let's discuss the approach before jumping into code.

First, do a little research and share:

- What you want to change and why.
- How you think it could be implemented.
- A simple example, sketch, or explanation of the expected result.

After we agree on the direction, start the implementation.

The goal is not to add extra steps, but to make sure we are building the right thing and avoid spending time redoing large changes later.
